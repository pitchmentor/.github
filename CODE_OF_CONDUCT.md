# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want the Pitch Mentor community, in our repositories, on Discord and anywhere else we gather, to be welcoming,
respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- being kind, patient and constructive, especially with newcomers,
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences,
- giving and gracefully accepting feedback on the work, not the person,
- owning our mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention,
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks,
- public or private harassment,
- publishing others' private information, such as an address or email, without their explicit permission,
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this
code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they find harmful.

Report unacceptable behavior to **support@pitchmentor.net** or privately to a moderator on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/SkcxVEJ6wQ). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
